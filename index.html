Today's menu

Three courses, one question: how do cells build things?

Starter

Biomaterials

Materials that cells can live in. Gelatin, agar and alginate sit in kitchens and labs alike, so I start by asking what each one gives a cell.

  • Hydrogels
  • Gelatin
  • Alginate
Main course

Tissue engineering

Building working tissue from cells, a porous scaffold and the right signals. The scaffold decides where cells attach, how they line up and how far nutrients travel.

  • Scaffolds
  • Cell culture
  • Regeneration
Dessert

Organoids

Stem cells that organize themselves into miniature organ-like structures, usually grown in a dome of hydrogel. A small model of a whole organ.

  • Stem cells
  • 3D culture
  • Disease models
